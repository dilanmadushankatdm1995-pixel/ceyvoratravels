Essential Island Information

Read our insider advice prepared by local Sri Lankan destination managers to ensure a relaxed and culturally respectful holiday.

Visa & Entry (ETA)

Official Requirements

Most foreign nationals require an Electronic Travel Authorization (ETA) prior to arriving in Sri Lanka. The tourist visa typically grants 30 days of stay with double entry.

  • Passport must be valid for at least 6 months from arrival.
  • Return flight ticket and proof of sufficient funds.
  • Always apply via the official government portal.
Visit Official ETA Portal

Dual Monsoon Seasons

Year-Round Sunshine

Sri Lanka has a year-round tropical climate with two distinct monsoon patterns, ensuring one side of the island is always basking in sunshine:

☀️ Nov - April: Best for South & West Coast (Galle, Mirissa, Colombo) & Central Highlands.

☀️ May - Oct: Best for East Coast (Trincomalee, Arugam Bay, Pasikudah) & Cultural Triangle.

Coastal average: 27°C - 30°C | Hill Country: 15°C - 20°C.

Currency & Payments

Sri Lankan Rupee (LKR)

The local currency is the Sri Lankan Rupee (LKR). Major credit cards (Visa & MasterCard) are widely accepted in star-class hotels, supermarkets, and upscale restaurants.

  • ATMs are readily available in all commercial towns.
  • Keep small denomination rupee notes for village stalls and tuk-tuks.
  • Tipping: 10% is customary for restaurant service, and modest daily tips for chauffeur guides ($10-$15/day).

Temple Etiquette

Sacred Customs

Sri Lanka has deep Buddhist, Hindu, Christian, and Islamic roots. When entering sacred temple grounds:

Modest Dress: Shoulders and knees must be covered. White or light-colored clothing is respected.

Footwear & Hats: Must be removed before entering temple compounds.

Buddha Statue Respect: Never pose with your back turned to a Buddha image for photos.

Scenic Trains & Transport

Island Travel

Traveling by private vehicle is the most flexible way to tour Sri Lanka. However, the world-famous Kandy to Ella blue train is an unmissable highlight.

  • Ceyvora reserves observation-class train seats 30 days in advance.
  • Modern expressways connect Colombo Airport directly to Galle & Hambantota.
  • Use meter-taxis (PickMe or Uber) in Colombo and Kandy.

Health & Safety

Safe & Welcoming

Sri Lanka is considered one of South Asia’s safest travel destinations for solo travelers and families alike.

  • Drink sealed bottled or filtered water; avoid untreated tap water.
  • Carry tropical insect repellent for evening safari hours.
  • Excellent private hospital network in Colombo, Kandy, and Galle.
  • Travel insurance with medical coverage is highly recommended.
