Our Roots, Passion & Purpose

We are proud sons and daughters of Sri Lanka, dedicated to crafting deeply meaningful, authentic, and responsible island journeys.

Our Origin Story

Born from a Lifelong Love for Ceylon

Ceyvora Travels began over fifteen years ago in the emerald hillocks of Kandy with a humble mission: to show world travelers the Sri Lanka we grew up cherishing. Beyond the commercial tour buses, we longed to introduce guests to the ancient stone secrets of Sigiriya, the secluded leopard trails of Yala, and the warm hospitality of rural village kitchens.

Today, Ceyvora Travels has grown into an internationally respected, SLTDA-licensed Destination Management Company. Yet our core philosophy remains unchanged: every itinerary is tailor-crafted, every chauffeur is an accredited local storyteller, and every journey honors Sri Lanka's fragile ecosystems and centuries-old heritage.

Guiding Principles

Our Mission & Values

How we operate sets us apart. We believe tourism must enrich both the visitor and the island we call home.

Authentic Immersion

We connect travelers with genuine Sri Lankan life—sharing home-cooked curries with village families, meeting master cinnamon peelers, and learning traditional mask carving from multi-generational artisans.

Ethical Wildlife

We adhere strictly to non-invasive wildlife protocols. No elephant riding, no chained animal photo-ops, and respectful distance tracking in our national parks and marine reserves.

Fair Community Economy

Over 85% of trip revenue goes directly to local family-owned guesthouses, licensed community guides, safari jeep operators, and indigenous food growers across rural Sri Lanka.

Safety & Accountability

Full comprehensive commercial insurance, 24/7 GPS fleet monitoring, certified English-speaking drivers, and direct coordination with the Sri Lanka Tourist Police.

Sustainability Commitment

The Ceyvora Ethical Tourism Pledge

Sri Lanka is blessed with extraordinary biodiversity, being one of the world’s 36 recognized biodiversity hotspots. We treat this privilege with the utmost reverence.

Wild & Free Wildlife

We endorse observing Asian elephants solely in free-roaming national parks like Udawalawe and Minneriya. We firmly boycott captive elephant riding.

Marine Protection

Our whale watching cruises in Mirissa and Trincomalee maintain strict 100-meter minimum distances, avoiding speed chasing or pod disturbance.

Plastic-Conscious

We supply each guest with reusable copper water vessels and refill stations in our vehicles, preventing thousands of single-use bottles annually.
